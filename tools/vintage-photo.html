  2. Tune the look — grab more fade, warmth or grain to taste.

What makes a film look?

A true vintage film look isn't just one filter. It combines a few classic analog clues:

  • Faded blacks — lifted shadows that soften contrast.
  • Warm tones — yellow and red shifted highlights, like aged film.
  • Film grain — fine random noise from silver-halide crystals.
  • Vignette — darker corners from lens falloff.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it reduce image quality?

No. The effect is applied to the full-resolution pixels and you download the original size — nothing is upscaled or downsampled.

Can I undo the effect?

Yes — just lower the Strength slider back to 0 and download the untouched original, or pick a new image.

Is my data safe?

Fully safe — everything runs locally. No image is ever uploaded to a server.
