Frequently Asked Questions

Is this JPG compressor really free?

Yes. There are no limits, no sign-up, and no watermarks on your downloads.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. Your images never leave your device.

How much can I compress a JPG?

Most photos shrink by 50–90% at quality settings between 60 and 80, usually with no visible difference.

What quality setting should I use?

70–80% is ideal for websites and email. Use 85–95% if you plan to print the image or archive it.
