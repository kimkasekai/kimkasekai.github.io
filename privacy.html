Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

miniPhotoShop ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our online image editor.

Data Processing

miniPhotoShop is a client-side application. This means:

  • All image editing operations are performed entirely in your browser.
  • No images, files, or personal data are uploaded to or stored on any server.
  • Your images never leave your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require registration or login.
  • We do not collect email addresses, names, or any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not track your image editing activities.

Cookies

miniPhotoShop may use local storage in your browser to save your preferences (such as language settings and theme). This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to any external server.

Third-Party Services

miniPhotoShop may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Children's Privacy

miniPhotoShop does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at kimkasekai@gmail.com.
