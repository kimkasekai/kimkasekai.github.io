How to make an image background transparent

  1. Drop your image into the box above.
  2. Click the background you want to remove, then press Remove Background.
  3. Download the transparent PNG.

Tips for best results

  • Plain backgrounds work best — studio shots, product photos, ID pictures.
  • If too little disappears, increase the tolerance; if parts of your subject vanish, decrease it.
  • You can run the tool multiple times — click another leftover spot and remove again.
  • Made a mistake? Hit Undo to restore the previous state.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which images work best?

Solid or smoothly lit backgrounds — white, blue, green screens. Complex scenes (trees, crowds) need AI tools instead.

Does it keep quality?

Yes — pixels are never resized or recompressed beyond PNG encoding.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, even offline after the first load.

Is it free?

Yes — unlimited use, no sign-up, no watermarks.
