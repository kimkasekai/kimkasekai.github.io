How to merge photos

  1. Drop or select 2 to 9 photos using the box above.
  2. Pick a layout — side-by-side, grid, or vertical strip.
  3. Download the merged image as a PNG.

Available layouts

  • Side-by-side — place 2–3 photos in a horizontal row.
  • Grid 2×2 / 3×3 — arrange photos in a square grid.
  • Vertical strip — stack photos from top to bottom.

Why merge with miniPhotoShop?

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photos can I merge at once?

You can merge 2 to 9 photos. Layout options adjust automatically based on the number of images.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser. Your images never leave your device.

Can I change the order of the photos?

Yes. Click the X button to remove a photo, then re-add it in the order you want.

What format will the merged image be?

The output is a PNG file for maximum quality. You can convert it afterwards with our WebP converter.
