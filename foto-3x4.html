Tips for a good ID photo

  • Use a plain background with even lighting.
  • Face the camera with a neutral expression and open eyes.
  • Avoid hats or accessories that cover your face (except for religious reasons).
  • Make sure your face fills about 60–70% of the frame.
  • For official documents, always check the background color requirements of the issuing agency — some require red or blue backgrounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is a 3x4 photo in pixels?

A 3x4 cm photo is 354 × 472 pixels at 300 DPI — the sharp standard print resolution.

Can I print the result directly?

Yes. The file is exported at 300 DPI, so it stays sharp when printed at its original size.

Is my photo safe?

Yes. Everything happens in your own browser — your photo is never uploaded to any server.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Just open this page in your phone or laptop browser and everything works right away.
