Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert to WebP?

WebP produces files 30–70% smaller than JPG/PNG at the same visual quality — supported by every modern browser since 2020.

Can I convert WebP back to JPG?

Yes — many programs still struggle with WebP, so just drop it here and pick JPG.

What happens to transparency when converting to JPG?

JPG cannot store transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white automatically.

Is my data safe?

Fully safe — conversion runs locally in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
