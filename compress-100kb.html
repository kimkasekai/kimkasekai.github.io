Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image to exactly 100KB?

Just drop the image and keep the default target of 100KB. The tool tests many JPEG quality levels automatically and keeps the highest one that fits under 100KB.

What if the image stays bigger than the target?

If quality reduction alone cannot reach the target, the image dimensions are gently reduced step by step until it fits.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser using JavaScript — your images never leave your device.

Which formats are supported?

You can load JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP. The output is always JPG, which gives the smallest sizes for photos and forms.
