miniPhotoShop - Free Online Photo Editor in Your Browser

miniPhotoShop is a free web-based image editor built with HTML5 and JavaScript. Everything runs locally on your device — your photos are never uploaded to a server. You can edit images, apply photography effects and filters, work with layers, remove backgrounds, resize and crop, add text overlays, and export the final result in JPG, PNG, or WebP format without installing any software.

What can you do with the online image editor?

  • Resize and crop images to exact dimensions.
  • Apply photography effects, filters, and color corrections.
  • Remove backgrounds and create transparent images.
  • Use layers, masks, and blend modes for advanced editing.
  • Add text, shapes, and watermarks to protect your photos.
  • Compress JPG and PNG files, and convert images to WebP.
  • Restore and touch up your own personal photos.

How to edit a photo online

  1. Open a photo with File → Open, or drag and drop an image onto the canvas.
  2. Choose a tool from the left sidebar: brush, selection, text, effects, fill, and more.
  3. Adjust your edits from the panels on the right, then preview the result.
  4. Save with File → Save to download your image in JPG, PNG, or WebP.

Frequently asked questions

Is miniPhotoShop really free? Yes — no account is required, there are no usage limits, and we never add watermarks to your downloaded files.

Are my photos uploaded to a server? No. All image processing happens in your browser using the HTML5 canvas API, so your pictures stay on your own device.

Which file formats are supported? You can open popular formats such as JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG, and BMP, and export to JPG, PNG, or WebP.
