miniPhotoShop - Free Online Image Editor

About miniPhotoShop

miniPhotoShop is a free online image editor that runs entirely in your browser. No downloads, no installations, no sign-ups required. Open your photos, edit them with professional tools, and save the results directly to your computer. Built with HTML5 and JavaScript, it works on any device with a modern web browser.

Key Features

  • Multi-Layer Editing - Work with multiple layers just like professional desktop software. Reorder, duplicate, merge, and adjust layer opacity for complex compositions.
  • Filters and Effects - Apply over 30 built-in effects including blur, sharpen, sepia, vintage, grayscale, vignette, emboss, and Instagram-style filters with one click.
  • Professional Drawing Tools - Paint with brushes, pencils, gradients, shapes, and text. Use the clone stamp, magic eraser, and color picker for precise editing.
  • Color Corrections - Adjust brightness, contrast, hue, saturation, and luminance. Auto-fix photos, correct white balance, and fine-tune colors with live preview.
  • Resize and Crop - Resize images to any dimension, crop with custom aspect ratios, rotate, flip, and trim transparent edges automatically.
  • File Format Support - Import and export PNG, JPG, BMP, WEBP, GIF, and TIFF files. Save projects for later editing and export high-quality results.

How It Works

  1. Open your image - Drag and drop a file or click to browse your computer. You can also paste images from clipboard.
  2. Edit with tools - Use the toolbar on the left to select drawing tools, filters, and effects. Adjust settings in the top bar.
  3. Manage layers - Add, remove, and reorder layers in the right sidebar. Control visibility and opacity for each layer.
  4. Save your work - Export your edited image as PNG, JPG, or other formats. Your images never leave your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is miniPhotoShop really free?

Yes, miniPhotoShop is completely free to use. There are no hidden fees, no premium plans, and no watermarks on your exported images.

Do my images get uploaded to a server?

No. All editing happens directly in your browser. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

What file formats are supported?

miniPhotoShop supports PNG, JPG, BMP, WEBP, GIF, TIFF, and SVG files for import. You can export your edited images as PNG, JPG, or BMP.

Does it work on mobile phones?

miniPhotoShop is responsive and works on tablets and phones. For the best editing experience, we recommend using a desktop or laptop computer.

Can I use it without internet?

After the first page load, miniPhotoShop can work offline. The entire application runs in your browser without needing a server connection.

Why Choose miniPhotoShop?

Unlike other online editors that require accounts, subscriptions, or uploads, miniPhotoShop gives you a full-featured image editor instantly. It is open-source, privacy-focused, and built with modern web technologies. Whether you need to resize a photo, remove a background, apply filters, or create graphics for social media, miniPhotoShop handles it all without leaving your browser.
