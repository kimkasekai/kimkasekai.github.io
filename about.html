About Us

What is miniPhotoShop?

miniPhotoShop is a free online image editor that lets you create and edit images directly in your browser using HTML5 technologies. No installation required, no downloads needed, and no account necessary. Just open the editor and start working on your images immediately.

Our Mission

We believe everyone should have access to powerful image editing tools without paying for expensive software or compromising their privacy. miniPhotoShop was built to provide a fast, free, and private editing experience that works on any device with a web browser.

Features

  • Multi-layer editing system - Work with multiple layers, adjust opacity, blend modes, and reorder layers for complex compositions.
  • Filters and effects - Over 30 built-in effects including blur, sharpen, sepia, vintage, grayscale, vignette, emboss, and Instagram-style filters.
  • Professional drawing tools - Brushes, pencils, gradients, shapes, text overlays, clone stamp, magic eraser, and color picker.
  • Color corrections - Adjust brightness, contrast, hue, saturation, luminance, auto-fix, and white balance.
  • Resize and crop - Resize to any dimension, crop with custom aspect ratios, rotate, flip, and trim transparent edges.
  • File format support - Import and export PNG, JPG, BMP, WEBP, GIF, TIFF, and SVG files.
  • 100% private - All editing happens in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

Open Source

miniPhotoShop is built on top of the open-source miniPaint project. We have customized and extended it with additional features, improved the user interface, and optimized it for modern web browsers. The original project is licensed under MIT, and we continue to contribute back to the community.

Privacy First

Your privacy matters. miniPhotoShop operates entirely in your browser. No images or data are uploaded or sent to any server. Everything stays on your device. We do not collect personal information, and we do not require registration or login.
